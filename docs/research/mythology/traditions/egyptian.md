# Egyptian Mythology

Source: mythologies.wiki (127 entries), ACP (17 Netjeru), comparative sources

## Key Themes
- Ma'at (cosmic order, truth, balance, justice) vs Isfet (chaos)
- Solar cycle - Ra battles Apep nightly
- Afterlife judgment - heart weighed against feather of truth
- Osiris resurrection cycle
- Divine kingship - pharaoh as Horus/Osiris
- Heka (magic) as creative force
- Cyclical renewal (not one-time creation)

## Pantheon Structure
**Ennead of Heliopolis** (9 gods):
- Atum/Atum-Ra (creator, emerged from Nun)
- Shu (air), Tefnut (moisture)
- Geb (earth), Nut (sky)
- Osiris, Isis, Set, Nephthys

**Other Major Deities**:
- Ra (sun, merged with Atum, Amun-Ra)
- Osiris (underworld, resurrection, kingship)
- Isis (magic, motherhood, throne)
- Horus (sky, kingship, falcon)
- Set (chaos, desert, storms, foreign lands)
- Thoth (wisdom, writing, moon, magic)
- Anubis (embalming, guide to afterlife)
- Hathor (love, beauty, music, sky)
- Sekhmet (war, destruction, healing)
- Bastet (protection, cats, home)
- Ptah (crafts, creation by word)
- Amun (hidden, later Amun-Ra)
- Ma'at (goddess of truth/order)
- Sobek (crocodile, Nile, fertility)
- Khonsu (moon, time)

## Notable Creatures
- Apep/Apophis (chaos serpent)
- Sphinx (lion-human, riddles)
- Bennu (phoenix, soul of Ra)
- Ammit (devourer of unworthy hearts)
- Serpopard (leopard-serpent)
- Uraeus (cobra on crown)
- Mummy (preserved body for afterlife)

## Shared Motifs
- Flood: Not prominent; Nile inundation is annual renewal
- Dying God: Osiris (murdered, resurrected, rules underworld)
- World Tree: Persea tree, Ished tree; not central axis mundi
- Trickster: Set (chaos, but not pure trickster)
- Underworld Journey: Duat (12 hours), Book of the Dead spells
