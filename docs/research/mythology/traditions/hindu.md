# Hindu Mythology

Source: mythologies.wiki (379 entries), ACP (15 Trimurti/Devas), comparative sources

## Key Themes
- Dharma (cosmic order/duty), Karma (action/consequence), Moksha (liberation)
- Trimurti: Brahma (Creator), Vishnu (Preserver), Shiva (Destroyer/Transformer)
- Avatar - divine descent (Vishnu's 10 avatars)
- Cosmic cycles (Yugas: Satya, Treta, Dvapara, Kali)
- Bhakti (devotion), Jnana (knowledge), Karma Yoga
- Atman/Brahman (self/ultimate reality)
- Maya (illusion), Samsara (rebirth cycle)

## Pantheon Structure
**Trimurti:**
- Brahma (Creator, 4 heads, Saraswati consort)
- Vishnu (Preserver, Lakshmi consort, 10 avatars: Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Parashurama, Rama, Krishna, Buddha, Kalki)
- Shiva (Destroyer/Transformer, Parvati consort, Nataraja dancing)

**Major Deities:**
- Ganesha (remover of obstacles, wisdom)
- Kartikeya/Skanda (war, victory)
- Durga/Kali/Parvati (Shakti, feminine power)
- Lakshmi (wealth, prosperity)
- Saraswati (knowledge, arts)
- Hanuman (devotion, strength)
- Surya (sun), Chandra (moon)
- Agni (fire), Vayu (wind), Varuna (cosmic order)
- Indra (king of devas, thunder, rain)
- Yama (death, justice)

**Asuras (Anti-gods):**
- Ravana, Hiranyakashipu, Bali, etc.

## Notable Creatures
- Naga (serpent beings)
- Garuda (eagle mount of Vishnu)
- Nandi (bull mount of Shiva)
- Airavata (elephant of Indra)
- Kamadhenu (wish-fulfilling cow)
- Gandharvas, Apsaras (celestial musicians/dancers)
- Rakshasas, Yakshas (nature spirits)

## Shared Motifs
- Flood: Manu saved by Matsya (fish avatar of Vishnu)
- Dying God: Not central; cyclical destruction/renewal via Shiva
- World Tree: Kalpavriksha (wish-fulfilling), Ashvattha (Bodhi)
- Trickster: Krishna (childhood), Narada (sage troublemaker)
- Underworld Journey: Yama's realm, Nachiketa
