# Persian / Zoroastrian Mythology

Source: mythologies.wiki (57 entries), ACP (18 Persian), comparative sources

## Key Themes
- Cosmic dualism: Ahura Mazda (Wise Lord) vs Angra Mainyu (Destructive Spirit)
- Free will and moral choice (asha vs druj)
- Fire as sacred symbol (truth, purity)
- Apocalyptic renewal (Frashokereti)
- Resurrection and final judgment
- Purity and pollution
- Heroic kingship
- Prophetic revelation (Zoroaster)

## Pantheon Structure
**Amesha Spentas (Bounteous Immortals - aspects of Ahura Mazda):**
- Vohu Manah (Good Mind)
- Asha Vahishta (Best Truth/Order)
- Khshathra Vairya (Desirable Dominion)
- Spenta Armaiti (Holy Devotion/Serenity)
- Haurvatat (Wholeness/Health)
- Ameretat (Immortality)

**Yazatas (Worthy of Worship):**
- Mithra (covenant, sun, justice)
- Anahita (waters, fertility, wisdom)
- Verethragna (victory)
- Tishtrya (rain, Sirius)
- Sraosha (obedience, prayer)
- Rashnu (judge of souls)
- Atar (fire)

**Opposition:**
- Angra Mainyu / Ahriman (destructive spirit)
- Daevas (false gods)
- Druj (the Lie)

## Notable Creatures
- Simurgh (benevolent bird, wisdom)
- Chamrosh (bird of mountains)
- Kar fish (guardians of water)
- Azhi Dahaka (three-headed dragon, Zahhak)

## Shared Motifs
- Flood: Yima (Jamshid) builds vara (enclosure) for survivors
- Dying God: Not central; Saoshyant (saviors) born of virgin
- World Tree: Gaokerena (white haoma, tree of life)
- Trickster: Not prominent; Ahriman as cosmic deceiver
- Underworld Journey: Chinvat Bridge (judgment), House of Song/House of Lies

## Game Design Notes
- Dualism as core alignment (asha/druj, not good/evil)
- Fire as resource/ritual focus
- Moral choices affect cosmic balance
- Apocalyptic timeline (3000-year cycles)
- Purity mechanics (pollution/purification)
- Chinvat Bridge as death mechanic
