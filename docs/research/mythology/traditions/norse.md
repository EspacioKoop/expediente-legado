# Norse Mythology

Source: mythologies.wiki, ACP (12 Aesir/Vanir), comparative sources, Poetic/Prose Edda

## Key Themes
- Ragnarök (prophecied end and rebirth)
- Wyrd (fate) - immutable even for gods
- Warrior ethos and heroic death
- Cosmic tree Yggdrasil connecting nine realms
- Magic (seiðr, runes) and wisdom-seeking
- Honor, oaths, and hospitality

## Pantheon Structure
**Aesir** (principal gods):
- Odin (Allfather, wisdom, war, death, poetry, runes)
- Thor (thunder, strength, protection, hallowing)
- Frigg (marriage, motherhood, foresight)
- Baldr (light, purity, beauty, reborn after Ragnarök)
- Tyr (law, justice, war, sacrifice of hand)
- Heimdall (watchman, horn Gjallar, senses)
- Bragi (poetry, music)
- Idunn (youth, golden apples)
- Ullr (hunting, archery, winter)
- Hodr (blindness, winter, slayer of Baldr)
- Váli (avenger of Baldr)
- Víðarr (silent god, avenger, survives Ragnarök)

**Vanir** (fertility, wisdom, associated with Vanir):
- Njord (sea, wind, fishing, wealth)
- Freyr (fertility, prosperity, sunshine, fair weather)
- Freyja (love, beauty, fertility, war, death, seiðr)
- Nerthus (earth fertility, Tacitus)

**Others**:
- Loki (trickster, shape-shifter, father of monsters)
- Giants (Jotnar) - often adversaries
- Valkyries (choosers of the slain)
- Norns (Urd, Verdandi, Skuld - weave fate)
- Disir (female ancestral spirits)
- Elves (light and dark), Dwarves (smiths)

## Notable Creatures
- Fenrir (wolf, destined to kill Odin)
- Jörmungandr (World Serpent, encircles Midgard)
- Hel (ruler of Helheim, half-dead)
- Sleipnir (Odin's eight-legged horse)
- Ratatoskr (squirrel on Yggdrasil)
- Níðhöggr (dragon gnawing Yggdrasil roots)
- Gullinbursti (Freyr's golden boar)
- Hildisvíni (Freyja's boar)
- Eagles, hawks, ravens (Huginn and Muninn)
- Wolves (Geri and Freki, Odin's)
- Dragons (Fafnir, etc.)

## Cosmology
- **Nine Realms** connected by Yggdrasil:
  - Asgard (Aesir)
  - Vanaheim (Vanir)
  - Midgard (humans)
  - Jotunheim (giants)
  - Niflheim (ice, mist, Helheim)
  - Muspelheim (fire, Surtr)
  - Alfheim (light elves)
  - Svartalfheim (dwarves)
  - Helheim (realm of dead, not heroic)
- Creation: Ginnungagap (void) between fire and ice, Ymir slain, body forms world
- End: Ragnarök - final battle, Surtr burns world, then rebirth

## Shared Motifs
- Flood: Bergelmir survives in a lore chest (parallel)
- Dying God: Baldr (killed by mistletoe, returns after Ragnarök)
- World Tree: Yggdrasil (axis mundi)
- Trickster: Loki (shape-shifting, causes Baldr's death, father of monsters)
- Underworld Journey: Hermod rides to Hel to beg for Baldr, others visit via cave

## Game Design Notes
- Norse cosmos is temporary and doomed; values courage in face of inevitable end
- Gods are not omnipotent; rely on wisdom, magic, fate
- Afterlife varies: Valhalla (chosen slain), Folkvangr (Freyja's), Helheim (sickness/old age), Ran's hall (drowned)
- Magic (seiðr) associated with femininity, Odin learns it
- Runes for divination and magic
- Oaths sworn on rings or holy places are binding
