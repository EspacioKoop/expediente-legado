# Yoruba Mythology (West Africa)

Source: mythologies.wiki (48 entries), ACP (16 Orisha), comparative sources

## Key Themes
- Orishas as intermediaries between Olodumare (supreme) and humanity
- Àṣẹ (life force, power to make things happen)
- Ifá divination system (16 major odù, 256 total)
- Ancestor veneration (Egun)
- Crossroads (Elegua) as liminal space
- Sacrifice (ebọ) and offering
- Diaspora religions: Santería (Cuba), Candomblé (Brazil), Vodou (Haiti)

## Pantheon Structure
**Major Orishas:**
- Olodumare (supreme creator, distant)
- Olofi (aspect of Olodumare, closer)
- Obatala (creator of bodies, wisdom, purity)
- Ogun (iron, war, technology, clearing paths)
- Ọya (wind, storms, marketplace, death)
- Ọshun (love, beauty, fertility, rivers, honey)
- Ọbaluaye/Omolu (healing, disease, earth)
- Shango (thunder, fire, justice, dance)
- Yemoja/Yemaya (motherhood, ocean, mother of fish)
- Orunmila (wisdom, knowledge, Ifá divination)
- Elegua/Eshu (trickster, crossroads, messenger)
- Osoosi (hunter, forest, justice)
- Ochosi (same as Osoosi)
- Babalu Aye (healer)
- Nana Buruku (primordial swamp, grandmother)
- Ibeji (twins, joy)
- Olokun (deep ocean, wealth)

## Notable Creatures & Concepts
- Àṣẹ (life force)
- Iwa Pele (good character)
- Ori (personal destiny)
- Egungun (ancestor masquerade)
- Gelede (spectacle, mothers)
- Opon Ifá (divination tray)
- Ikin (sacred palm nuts)
- Opele (divination chain)

## Shared Motifs
- Flood: Not central
- Dying God: Not central; Olokun as deep ocean deity
- World Tree: Iroko (sacred tree)
- Trickster: Elegua (crossroads), sometimes Tortoise
- Underworld Journey: Not emphasized; focus on ancestor realm (Orun)
