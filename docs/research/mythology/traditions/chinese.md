# Chinese Mythology

Source: mythologies.wiki (185 entries), ACP (19 Chinese pantheon), comparative sources

## Key Themes
- Celestial bureaucracy mirroring imperial government
- Yin-Yang cosmic balance
- Ancestral veneration
- Immortality cultivation (xian)
- Filial piety
- Mandate of Heaven
- Harmony with nature
- Transformation (animals, objects gaining sentience)

## Pantheon Structure
**Celestial Bureaucracy:**
- Jade Emperor (Yu Huang) - supreme ruler of heaven
- Three Pure Ones (Sanqing) - highest Daoist deities
- Four Heavenly Kings
- Twenty-Eight Mansions (lunar lodges)
- City Gods, Earth Gods, Kitchen Gods (local)

**Major Deities:**
- Pangu (creator, body becomes world)
- Nüwa (creator of humans, patches sky)
- Fuxi (culture hero, writing, fishing, marriage)
- Shennong (agriculture, medicine)
- Yellow Emperor (Huangdi, civilization founder)
- Xiwangmu (Queen Mother of West, immortality)
- Guan Yu (war, loyalty, deified general)
- Mazu (sea goddess)
- Caishen (wealth god)
- Zao Shen (Kitchen God)
- Tu Di Gong (Earth God)

**Eight Immortals (Ba Xian):**
- Zhongli Quan, Lü Dongbin, Zhang Guolao, Cao Guojiu, Li Tieguai, Han Xiangzi, Lan Caihe, He Xiangu

## Notable Creatures
- Dragon (Long) - celestial, water, imperial power
- Phoenix (Fenghuang) - grace, empress
- Qilin - benevolent, appears for sage rulers
- Pixiu - wealth attractor
- Jiangshi (hopping vampire)
- Huli Jing (fox spirits)
- Monkey King (Sun Wukong) - trickster, rebel

## Shared Motifs
- Flood: Yu the Great engineers flood control (not escape)
- Dying God: Not central; immortality cultivation
- World Tree: Fusang (mulberry tree of sun), Jianmu (connects heaven/earth)
- Trickster: Sun Wukong (Monkey King), Fox spirits
- Underworld Journey: Diyu (10 courts of hell), Meng Po's tea

## Game Design Notes
- Bureaucracy as cosmology: ranks, paperwork, promotion
- Cultivation systems (Qi, meridians, dantian, tribulations)
- Five Elements (Wu Xing) as magic/elemental system
- Karma/merit system for afterlife judgment
- Transformation mechanics (beast → human → immortal)
