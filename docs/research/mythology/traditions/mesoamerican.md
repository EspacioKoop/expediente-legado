# Mesoamerican Mythology (Aztec, Maya, Olmec)

Source: mythologies.wiki (81 Maya + Aztec entries), ACP (17 Mesoamerican), comparative sources

## Key Themes
- Duality (life/death, order/chaos, male/female)
- Sacrifice sustains the cosmos (tonalli, life force)
- Five Suns / World Ages (creations and destructions)
- Calendar cycles (tonalpohualli 260-day, xiuhpohualli 365-day)
- Feathered Serpent (Quetzalcoatl/Kukulkan)
- Underworld journeys (Mictlan, Xibalba)
- Bloodletting and auto-sacrifice

## Aztec Pantheon
- Ometeotl (dual creator, Ometecuhtli/Omecihuatl)
- Huitzilopochtli (sun, war, patron of Tenochtitlan)
- Quetzalcoatl (wind, wisdom, feathered serpent, Venus)
- Tezcatlipoca (night, sorcery, destiny, jaguar)
- Tlaloc (rain, lightning, agriculture)
- Chalchiuhtlicue (water, rivers, lakes)
- Xipe Totec (flaying, renewal, spring)
- Coatlicue (earth mother, stars)
- Mictlantecuhtli (death, underworld)
- Mictecacihuatl (lady of dead)

## Maya Pantheon
- Itzamna (creator, sky, writing)
- Kukulkan (feathered serpent, wind)
- Chaac (rain, lightning)
- Ix Chel (moon, fertility, medicine)
- Ah Puch (death)
- Hunahpu & Xbalanque (Hero Twins)
- Gucumatz (feathered serpent)
- Bacabs (four sky bearers)

## Notable Creatures
- Ahuizotl (water dog)
- Cipactli (crocodile earth monster)
- Nagual (shapeshifter)
- Alux (Maya forest spirits)
- Feathered serpents
- Jaguars (power, night)

## Shared Motifs
- Flood: Several world destructions by flood
- Dying God: Quetzalcoatl's descent/return, Hero Twins
- World Tree: Ceiba (Wacah Chan) connects 13 heavens, earth, 9 underworlds
- Trickster: Huehuecoyotl (coyote), Hero Twins
- Underworld Journey: Hero Twins in Xibalba, Mictlan's 9 levels

## Game Design Notes
- Five Suns as world state/era system
- Calendar as event scheduler (260/365 day cycles)
- Sacrifice mechanics (blood, hearts, auto-sacrifice)
- Tonalli (life force) as resource
- Duality as core alignment (not good/evil but balance)
- Underworld as 9-level dungeon
