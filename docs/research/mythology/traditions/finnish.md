# Finnish Mythology (Kalevala)

Source: mythologies.wiki (54 entries), ACP (14 Kalevala), comparative sources

## Key Themes
- Magic songs (runos) shape reality
- Sampo (mill of plenty - flour, salt, gold)
- North vs South (Kalevala vs Pohjola)
- Shamanic journeys
- Origin knowledge (words have power)
- Eternal sages (Väinämöinen)
- Nature spirits
- Bear cult

## Pantheon/Characters
- Väinämöinen (eternal sage, singer, shaman)
- Ilmarinen (smith, forger of Sampo)
- Lemminkäinen (warrior, womanizer, resurrected)
- Joukahainen (rival singer)
- Louhi (mistress of Pohjola, North)
- Aino (drowned maiden)
- Kullervo (tragic hero, suicide)
- Marjatta (virgin mother)
- Ukko (sky/thunder god, later Christianized)

## Notable Creatures
- Sampo (magical artifact)
- Swan of Tuonela
- Pike with Väinämöinen's knife
- Bear (sacred ancestor)
- Hiisi (sacred groves/spirits)
- Haltija (guardian spirits)

## Shared Motifs
- Flood: Not central
- Dying God: Lemminkäinen (dismembered, resurrected by mother)
- World Tree: Not prominent; cosmic pillar
- Trickster: Väinämöinen (wisdom tricks), Lemminkäinen
- Underworld Journey: Väinämöinen visits Tuonela
